Remove the unused external terminal launcher, window focus service, and terminal types from the public API. The removed exports are `ExternalTerminalLauncher`, `getExternalTerminalLauncher`, `resetExternalTerminalLauncher`, `WindowFocusService`, `getWindowFocusService`, `resetWindowFocusService`, `TerminalType`, `InteractiveSessionStatus`, `InteractiveSession`, `ExternalTerminalLaunchOptions`, `ExternalTerminalLaunchResult`, and `WindowFocusResult`.
